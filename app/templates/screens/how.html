What it connects

Redemption amounts, exit loads, taxes, goals and borrowing already exist in separate places. Withdrawal Copilot connects them around one question: I need this much. What happens if I take it out?

What it never does

Execute transactions, pick stocks, predict markets, recommend a product, or make withdrawing harder.

Coverage

Now: stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, fixed deposits and linked accessible cash. Later: bonds, NPS, PPF, gold and SGBs, REITs and InvITs, ULIPs.
